The Business Cycle

An Austrian Economics Simulator

  1. ⚙️ Steal manhole covers. Stand on one and press E. Don't let anyone see you.
  2. 🏗️ Sell them to a junkyard. Cantillon Scrap, next to the Fed, hears the news first. Last-in-Line Recycling finds out 12 seconds later.
  3. 💊 Buy drugs at the Corner before your fix runs out. Heroin lasts, crack makes you fast.
  4. 🦺 Wait for the city to replace them, then steal them again. Go all the way around for a bonus.
  5. 🏛️ Watch the Fed. Booms pay double for scrap and bring new manholes. Busts crash the price and the crews stop coming. Save covers in your squat for the boom.
