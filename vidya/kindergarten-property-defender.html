Kindergarten Property Defender

“Jack, you should share your toys.” Jack has other ideas.

✊ Red sleeve, grabby fist The teacher, redistributing your blocks. SLAP IT
🖐️ Green sleeve, open hand A classmate with something to trade: “🧃 for 2🧱”, or “3🧱 for your 🧃”. LET IT THROUGH
  • 👆 Tap or click a hand to slap it. Catch a thief on its way out and you get your blocks back.
  • 🤝 Trades happen when the hand reaches your pile. Buy things, then sell them to the next kid for more.
  • ⭐ Slapping a classmate who came to trade is aggression: you lose a gold star. Lose them all and you're sent to the principal.
  • 🧱 Lose all your blocks and your property is seized.
  • 🏫 Each school day has five periods: circle time, snack time, recess, nap time (lights out!) and show and tell. A new troublemaker turns up most days, and the Principal visits on Fridays.
  • 🧤 Some hands need more than a slap: right-click Oven Mitts (double-tap on a phone), and press and hold on Sticky Fingers.
  • 🎒 What you trade for can be used: tap it in your stash (or press its number). A cookie freezes every thief, juice makes your slaps reach farther, crayons build a fence…
  • 📢 Every thief you stop fills your meter. When it's full, press Space (or tap it) for PROPERTY RIGHTS!: every grabby hand gets blown away.
  • 📅 A bigger boss every Friday: the Principal, the Superintendent, the School Board, the Department of Education…
  • 💵 Watch out for kids in disguise: the Monopoly-money kid pays in funny money, and the Free Lunch kid’s “free” stuff costs a big handful. Slap them. But never slap the 🍎 Teacher’s Pet (it tells on you): pinch it.
  • 🧢 Get to know the regulars: Timmy is fair, Becky haggles, Dave buys what you’ve got. Trade with one four times and you’re best friends. Slap one and they sulk for days.
  • 🏖️ Every weekend, pick a perk for Jack. Survive five weeks and you graduate.
  • 🚌 Now and then, a special day: a substitute who mixes up the sleeves, picture day, a field trip, a snow day…
  • 🏗️ After school, spend blocks: a lemonade stand, a piggy bank, a No Trespassing sign, a Gadsden flag, hired security… A bigger pile draws more hands.
