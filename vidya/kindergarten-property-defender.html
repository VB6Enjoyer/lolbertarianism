“Jack, you should share your toys.” Jack has other ideas.

✊ Red sleeve, grabby fist The teacher, redistributing your blocks. SLAP IT
🖐️ Green sleeve, open hand A classmate with something to trade: “🧃 for 2🧱”, or “3🧱 for your 🧃”. LET IT THROUGH
  • 👆 Tap or click a hand to slap it. Catch a thief on its way out and you get your blocks back.
  • 🤝 Trades happen when the hand reaches your pile. Buy things, then sell them to the next kid for more.
  • ⭐ Slapping a classmate who came to trade is aggression: you lose a gold star. Lose them all and you're sent to the principal.
  • 🧱 Lose all your blocks and your property is seized.
  • 🏫 Each school day has five periods: circle time, snack time, recess, nap time (lights out!) and show and tell. A new troublemaker turns up most days, and the Principal visits on Fridays.
  • 💵 Watch out for the Monopoly-money kid: looks like a trade, pays in funny money. Fraud is aggression, so slap it.
  • 🏗️ After school, spend blocks: a lemonade stand, a piggy bank, a No Trespassing sign, a Gadsden flag, hired security… A bigger pile draws more hands.
