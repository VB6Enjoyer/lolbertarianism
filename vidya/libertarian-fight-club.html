        All the rules
        • 🦞 Every lobster is a faction: Ancap, Minarchist, Austrian, Chicago, Objectivist, Georgist, Agorist, Hoppean, Bleeding-Heart, Mises Caucus.
        • 🗣️ Fights are debates: three rounds of arguments. Each faction is weak to one kind of argument (×1.5) and shrugs off another (×0.65).
        • 📊 Read the tale of the tape: claw, shell, stamina, wits, record and form. Some lobsters hide a trait; once you've seen it, it's on the card.
        • 💸 It's pool betting: the crowd sets the odds. 😱 Screaming Susan backs whoever's popular, 😏 Squatting Sheila the underdog, 🤪 LMAO Larry whatever's funniest, and the rest follow the stats while only half-reading the matchup chart.
        • ⏸ Between rounds the live market opens: bet on the fight as it stands, or cash out a bet early.
        • 🏟️ A career: five bouts a night, from the 🍳 Kitchen Floor to the 🚗 Garage, 🏕️ PorcFest, the 🏛️ Convention, and the 🏰 Liberland Championship. Go broke and you get thrown down a venue.
        • 🦈 Broke? Vinny the Fin lends at 20% a night, and collects after three unpaid nights. Or call your uncle and get thrown down a venue.
        • 🦞 Once you reach the Garage, buy a lobster of your own at the fish market, train it, and send it into the ring for purses.
        • 🎲 Props on how it ends, 🧾 parlays across tonight's card, and 🕵️ intel: buy tips (check their track records) or watch a lobster in the tank for tells.
        • 🌙 Some nights come with a twist: a tax holiday, a Bitcoin whale, a lockdown, a raid…
        • 🏆 Every fourth night is the Invisible Claw Cup: a knockout bracket of eight, with futures on the winner.
        • 🏁 Susan, Sheila and Larry keep score too. Top tonight's table, and the season's.
        • 🏢 Bigger venues have room for a stable of three: you see the opponent, then pick who fights it. Each lobster eats (upkeep), and can be sold or retired to the hall of fame.
        • 📚 Teach your lobster an argument its faction doesn't use, to hit more weak spots.
        • 🤫 Your own lobster can be paid to take a dive. The crowd won't know. The commission might.
        • ⌨️ Keys: 1/2 bet on a lobster, Space skips, continues, or goes to the next bout.
